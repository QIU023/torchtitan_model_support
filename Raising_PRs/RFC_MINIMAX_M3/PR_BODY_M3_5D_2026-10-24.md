Draft placeholder, not for merge: MiniMax M3 with MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13392) on FSDP2, TP/SP, EP and PP.

The first five commits are the FSDP2 baseline and the common changes it needs, which open as their own PRs once the full parallelism matrix has run; the last three add TP/SP, EP and PP.

## Summary
- `torchtitan/models/minimax_m3/`: the model (428B total, 23B activated), MSA on 57 of its 60 layers as a flex inner attention over the blocks an indexer selects, the indexer's KL loss, the vision tower with 3D RoPE, and the HF state dict adapter.
- `scripts/checkpoint_conversion/numerical_tests_minimax_m3.py`: parity against HF `minimax_m3_vl` on a reduced config.
- `torchtitan/models/common/`: `OffsetRMSNorm` moves here from Qwen3.5, `RoPE` applies partial RoPE itself, the DeepSeek V3 adapter takes a subclass's key map, Qwen3.5's post-vision-scatter block sharding config moves here, and one vision projector sharding config replaces the identical ones of Qwen3.5 and Kimi K2.7, which Kimi K3 also used.
- CP raises `NotImplementedError`: MSA's mask comes from a per-layer block selection that depends on the activations, which the CP flex path does not cover.

## Design
- TP for MSA:
  - The index query heads shard with their KV groups, and the shared index key head is replicated.
  - An MSA layer gathers its input once for the q/k/v and index projections, so its `wqkv` is a plain `Linear`.
  - The KL loss divides by the global KV group count, and its logged value sums every TP rank's groups.
- EP and PP use titan's MoE expert parallelism and pipeline split unchanged.

## Test plan
- `pytest tests/unit_tests/cpu` (`1932 passed, 18 skipped, 3 xfailed, 1 failed`; the one failure fails the same way on main) and `pytest tests/unit_tests/gpu/models/minimax_m3 -q` (`2 passed`)
- The models-suite cell `minimax_m3_mm_fsdp+tp+sp+ep` (FSDP 2 x TP 2 x EP 2, SPMD typechecking on) (`passed`)
- HF parity on the debug flavor in bf16: last-token KL 3.40e-5, and 4.86e-5 over all positions with HF's block and expert choices imposed.
- Pending: the parallelism matrix on 8 GPUs, every pair of FSDP/HSDP, TP and PP with EP on and off; its results land here.
